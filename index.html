Service Level & SLA Agreement

1. Scope & Not-To-Exceed (NTE): Work orders are dispatched under a strictly authorized labor limit. Technician will request check-out approval before exceeding authorized hours.

2. Deliverables & Closeout: NomadIT provides complete photo documentation of all installations, cable certification logs (Fluke scans if ordered), and signed client sign-off sheets upon job completion.

3. Arrival Window & SLA: Standard dispatch occurs within 24–48 hours. Emergency dispatches will have a technician on-site within 4 hours. 2-hour arrival windows apply.

4. Work Warranty: All physical cabling runs and hardware installations carry a 30-day labor workmanship warranty from date of ticket sign-off.
