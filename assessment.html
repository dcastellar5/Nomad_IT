The Pain of Unregulated Field Techs

⚠️ Low-Quality Workmanship

Many dispatch companies send unchecked gig-workers who arrive without critical tooling like ladder clamps, RJ45 crimpers, or fiber certifiers, leading to half-done installations and project delays.

⚠️ Missing Closeout Photos

Getting proof of deliverables is notoriously difficult. Sites are left with unlabeled ethernet runs, messy racks, and no photograhic documentation or signed ticket sheets.

⚠️ Unpredictable Invoice Padding

Without clear scope scoping and structured NTE (Not-to-Exceed) thresholds, single dispatches can result in bloated invoices, inflated travel fees, and double-billed labor hours.
